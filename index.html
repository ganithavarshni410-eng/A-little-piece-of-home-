01 · THE BEGINNING

Room 213

The girl from Room 213 ♡

11th grade. First hostel. 10 girls in one room.

And then there was you.

Short hair. Big feelings. And a lot of tears. 😂
I used to come back from the bathroom and hear you crying.

At that point, I definitely didn't know you'd become one of my favourite people.

Little did I know that the crying girl in Room 213 would become my Joju. ♡

02 · THINGS I REMEMBER FOR NO REASON

Honestly… we were something. 😂

Me pulling your tiny ponytail.
You shouting at me for it. 😂
“Thoongitiyaa?” at night.
That one month we didn't talk. 💀
Me writing “Deepa” instead of “deep water” in an exam.
And somehow… we survived each other.
03 · 2 AM
2:00 AM

“My head hurts.”

So there we were — third floor → ground floor → sick room.

The 2 AM adventure:

3rd floor ↓ stairs ↓ ground floor ↓ sick room

You went back to sleep.

I stayed awake until you did.

One of those tiny moments I still remember.

04 · THE THINGS I KEPT

Things I couldn't throw away ♡

The tiny pink keychain you gave me.

That rubber band. Yes, I actually kept it. 😂

The last letter. You have no idea how many times I've read it.

Maybe it's a little silly. But these tiny things somehow kept pieces of those days with me.

05 · ONE LITTLE PIECE OF US

Your fingerprint

12th grade.
Before Russia.
Before college.

We didn't know where life was taking us.

I was scared of losing you after hostel.

I remember wondering whether we'd still be the same once we left.

Then came the integrated NEET course, your hostel, my online classes, and eventually… distance.

We lost touch for a while.

Then you wished me on my birthday.

And I was literally on cloud nine.

I had missed you more than I knew how to say.

Later, I came to Russia, you started college, and somehow we found our way back to each other.

07 · FIFTEEN MINUTES

The 15 minute reunion

15:00 → 14:32 → 11:08 → 07:41 → 03:12 → 00:15

That's all the time we had.

During vacation, I came to your college.

We had only around 15 minutes together.

Both of us had tears in our eyes.

We talked, took photos, and somehow those few minutes felt much bigger than they actually were.

Sometimes 15 minutes is enough to make you miss someone all over again. ♡

08 · ONE MORE MEMORY

A tiny moving memory

A tiny moving piece of that day.

Something I can come back to whenever I miss those fifteen minutes.
