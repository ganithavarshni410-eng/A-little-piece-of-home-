01 / 08
ROOM 213 · 11TH GRADE

Before you became my person…

you were just the girl who cried every morning.

Our first hostel experience. Ten girls from different places. One room. And somehow, you were the girl I noticed.


Every morning I'd come back after my bath and everyone would be somewhere around the bathroom…

…but you would still be sitting on your lower bed, crying because you missed home.


That was my very first picture of you. Not a photograph. Just a memory.

02 / 08
12TH GRADE

And then… we became us.

Same room. Same person. Completely different friendship.

I annoyed you. You shouted at me. We fought over the most ridiculous things. I got possessive over absolutely nothing.

And somehow, between all of that, you became one of the most important people in my life.

The tiny ponytail incident. 😂

One of the little things we kept. 👆🏻

And yes…
I still remember writing “Deepa” instead of “deep water” in an exam and then desperately striking it out.

Apparently your name had already occupied too much space in my brain. 😭

03 / 08
02:00 AM · 3RD FLOOR → GROUND FLOOR

That night.

One night you woke me up because your head was hurting badly.


We went all the way down to the sick room. And when we came back, I stayed awake until you fell asleep.


It wasn't some dramatic moment. There was no big conversation.


I just remember realising how much you meant to me.


Maybe that was the night I stopped thinking of you as just my roommate.

04 / 08

The things I kept.

Some things are tiny. They still carry entire memories.

The little pink keychain you gave me. 🎀

I left the hostel three days before everyone else. I asked you to help me pack.

You said:
“I am going to sleep.”

Mean girl. 😭

But then you gave me this. And you hugged me.

And I saw the water in your eyes.

05 / 08

Little things.

Things nobody else would understand.

A rubber band you gave me.

I know this is such a tiny thing.

But somehow, I still have it.

Maybe that's the funny thing about memories. Sometimes the smallest things end up surviving the longest.

06 / 08

A letter.

Something you wrote that I couldn't bring myself to lose.

Your last letter to me. 💌

I don't think you realise how much meaning ordinary things can hold when they're connected to someone.

07 / 08

Different lives.

We left the hostel. Life got complicated. NEET happened. Stress happened. You went back to hostel. I was doing things online.


And for a while, we weren't the same two girls sharing Room 213 every day.


But I still missed you.


Then somehow, you wished me on my birthday.


And I was on cloud nine.


Later, I came to Russia. You started college. Life took us in completely different directions.


But somehow, we found each other again.

08 / 08

15 minutes.

I came to your college just to see you.

We had barely any time.
But somehow, we both had tears in our eyes.

After everything, there we were again.

We found our way back. ♡

Just 15 minutes.
