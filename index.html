11th grade • hostel days

The girl I met in our hostel room ♡

room 206 ♡
before we knew how much this friendship would mean
Short hair, big feelings, and a lot of tears. (you cried a lot okay?! 😂)
I would come back from the common bathroom after bathing and hear you crying.

I still remember the first time I really noticed you. We were both new to hostel life, surrounded by girls from different places, and somehow we ended up sharing the same little room.

At that time, we barely knew each other. You were just the girl who slept on the lower cot near the door.

I had absolutely no idea that this girl would later become someone I would miss this much. ❤️

one random night • 2:00 AM

The night I realised you mattered to me

2:00 AM

One night, around 2 AM, you woke me up because your head was hurting badly.

So we went from the 3rd floor, down the stairs, all the way to the ground-floor sick room.

I stayed awake until you finally fell asleep.

I don't think I realised it that night, but you had already become someone very important to me.

Just one of those tiny moments that somehow stayed with me. ♡

distance • growing up • finding our way back

Somehow, we found our way back ♡

I was scared of losing you after hostel. I kept wondering whether we'd still be the same once we left that place.
Then came the integrated NEET course, your hostel, my online classes… and somehow, distance.
We slowly lost touch.
Then you wished me on my birthday. I was literally on cloud nine.
I had missed my Joju more than I knew how to say.
Then I went to Russia. You joined college. Life became completely different for both of us.
And somehow… we found our way back.

Maybe some people really do find their way back. ♡

college reunion • one tiny visit

The 15 minute reunion

♡ 15 minutes. A whole memory.

During vacation, I came to your college.

We only had around 15 minutes together.

And somehow, both of us had tears in our eyes.

We took photos, laughed, probably didn't know what to say properly…

But those 15 minutes felt much bigger than 15 minutes.

It felt like all those hostel days, all that distance, and all the missing each other somehow fit into one tiny moment.
