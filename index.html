A little birthday memory book
To the girl from Room 213… ♡
Some memories disappear with time.
Some become a little piece of home.
Memory 01

Room 213

Before we became us, we were just two girls sharing a room.

Our first hostel experience. Ten girls from different places. And somehow, you became the girl I would remember.

The girl who would become such a big part of my life. ♡

You had short hair. You slept on the lower bed near the door. And you were always the girl crying because you missed home.

♡ ♡ ♡
Memory 02

Then came 12th...

Same class. Same room. And somehow, somewhere between annoying each other, sitting together and fighting over the smallest things... we became really close.

The tiny ponytail I used to pull just to hear you shout. 😂

I would come back from the common bathroom after bathing, see you sleeping and ask:

“Thoongitiyaa? 🤣”

We fought over silly things. We even went almost a whole month without talking. And yet somehow, we always found our way back.

♡ ♡ ♡
Memory 03

2:00 AM

One night, around 2 AM, you woke me up.

Your head was hurting badly. So we walked all the way from the third floor down to the sick room.

I stayed awake until you fell asleep.

I don't think I said it then, but somewhere that night I realised:

You mattered to me.

More than I had realised.

♡

And after that, I started wondering... “What if leaving hostel means losing her?”

♡ ♡ ♡
Memory 04

Little things I kept

Some things are tiny. But somehow they carry an entire memory inside them.

The tiny pink keychain you gave me. 🩷
A random rubber band that somehow became a keepsake.
The last letter you wrote me. Yes, I still kept it. 🥹
Memory 05

A fingerprint of us

We didn't have hundreds of pictures together.

So we made our own little proof that we were there.

One tiny piece of us from those days. ♡

Looking back at this now feels strangely precious.

♡ ♡ ♡
Memory 06

After everything...

Hostel ended. Then came the NEET course and the drop year. You went to hostel, I did mine online.

And slowly, life became busy. We lost touch.

But then you wished me on my birthday.

And I remember being completely on cloud nine.

Some people can disappear from your everyday life without disappearing from your heart.

Then I came to Russia. You started college. And somehow...

We found our way back.

Memory 07

Just 15 minutes.

When I came to your college during vacation, we had barely 15 minutes together.

But we both had tears in our eyes. And somehow those few minutes felt so full.

We found our way back. ♡
Just 15 minutes.

Not every important memory needs a whole day.

Memory 08

And then there was this...

A tiny video. A tiny moment. Another little piece of home.

♡ 🦋 ♡

If someone had told those two girls in Room 213 that years later they would still be making memories, I don't think we would have believed them.

For you
❤️

Happy 20th, Deepa

From Room 213 to different cities, different colleges, different lives...

somehow, you're still one of the people who feels like home.

Thank you for every silly fight, every laugh, every “don't disturb me”, every little thing you probably don't even remember.

I hope 20 brings you everything your heart has been quietly waiting for.

And wherever life takes us, I hope we always find our way back.

♡ ♡ ♡

Here's to the girl who started as my roommate and somehow became a little piece of home.

Always your annoying roommate ♡
