A little birthday memory book for my Joju

🧸❤️

To my Joju,
my teddy bear from Room 213…

Some memories disappear with time.
Some become a little piece of home.

This one is for you.

01 · THE BEGINNING

Room 213 🧸

Before you became my Joju, you were simply the girl in Room 213.

Two girls having their first hostel experience, surrounded by nine other girls from different places.

And somehow, among all those people, our story began there.

Little did I know that the crying girl in Room 213 would become my teddy bear. 🧸

02 · FIRST IMPRESSIONS

The crying girl 😂

You had short hair.

And you were the girl who always seemed to cry because you missed home.

I would come back from the common bathroom after bathing and hear you crying.

That was honestly one of my first memories of you.

Funny how the person I barely knew then became someone I would miss so much later.

03 · 2 AM

That night

In 11th grade, we were mostly just roommates.

But 12th grade changed everything.

Same class. Same room. More time together.

I would sit with you, annoy you, get possessive over you, and somehow we became inseparable.

We fought over the tiniest things too. There was even that one month when we didn't speak.

But one night, around 2 AM, you woke me because your head was hurting badly.

We walked from the third floor all the way down to the sick room.

I stayed awake until you slept.

And somewhere between that walk and waiting beside you, I realised just how much you meant to me.

04 · LITTLE THINGS

Things I couldn't throw away

The tiny pink keychain you gave me. 🎀

The rubber band you gave me. Yes, I actually kept it. 🥹

And the last letter you wrote.

Maybe it's silly. Maybe it's a little cringe. But these tiny things hold pieces of you.

05 · OUR LITTLE PROOF

One tiny piece of us

We didn't have many photos together.

But we had this.

Two fingerprints from 12th grade, from a time when we had no idea how many places life would take us.

Somehow, this little picture became one of my favourite pieces of us.

06 · DISTANCE

And then life happened…

I was scared of losing you after hostel.

I remember wondering whether we would still be the same once we left.

Then came the integrated NEET course, your hostel, my online classes, and eventually… distance.

We lost touch for a while.

But then you wished me on my birthday.

And I was literally on cloud nine.

I had missed my Joju more than I knew how to say.

Later, I came to Russia, you started college, and somehow we found our way back to each other.

07 · FIFTEEN MINUTES

Just 15 minutes

During vacation, I came to your college.

We had only around 15 minutes together.

But both of us had tears in our eyes.

We took these photos, stood together, talked, and somehow those few minutes felt so much bigger than they actually were.

Sometimes you don't need a whole day with someone to feel how much they mean to you.

08 · ONE MORE MEMORY

Us, for a little while ❤️

A tiny moving piece of that day.

Something I can come back to whenever I miss those fifteen minutes.

09 · FOR MY JOJU

Happy 20th,
my Joju 🧸❤️

From Room 213 to different cities, different colleges, different lives…

somehow, you're still one of the people who feels like home.

My teddy bear. 🧸
My annoying roommate.
My fight partner.
My Joju.

Thank you for every silly fight, every laugh, every little memory, and every moment that somehow stayed with me.

Thank you for being part of one of the most unforgettable chapters of my life.

I hope 20 brings you everything your heart has been quietly waiting for.

And wherever life takes us, I hope we always find our way back.

♡ 🧸 ♡

Here's to the girl who started as my roommate and somehow became a little piece of home.

Happy birthday, Joju 🧸❤️
